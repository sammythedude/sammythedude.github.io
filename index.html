Research

I am interested in parameter-efficient LLM alignment, hallucination detection, quantitative evaluation, uncertainty, calibration, and systems that make compact models more useful through retrieval, distillation, and tool use.

In applied vision, I work on label-free 3D holotomography pipelines for single-cell analysis, with an emphasis on interpretable features and translational validation.
